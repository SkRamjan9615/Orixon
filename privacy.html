ORIXON Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Package ID: org.orixon.app

1. Zero Telemetry & Local-First Storage

ORIXON does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data, watch history, local media library, or playlists to any tracking server. All settings, playlists, and watch progress are stored locally on your device in an SQLite database.

2. Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media: Required only to scan and play your local videos in Media Hub, browse files in File Hub, and export/restore JSON backups.
  • Microphone: Used strictly when you tap Universal Voice Search.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi: Used for your own M3U streams, Wi-Fi Web Portal (:8888), Wireless FTP (:2121), and Direct Beam transfers.
